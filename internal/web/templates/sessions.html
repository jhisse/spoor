How spoor tells the contexts apart, and what C, B and E mean

The spans do not say which context a call belongs to, so spoor reads it from the tokens: a call continues the earlier call whose prompt it read from cache; when it read none, spoor assumes the largest earlier prompt of the same model not larger than its own, and says so. C smaller context: a prompt smaller than every context so far — a compaction or a sub-agent's first call; the spans do not say which. B cache break: the call read at most half the cache share its context had and wrote at least as much as it read. E cache expiry: a break after 5 minutes or more without a call in that context. A pause is labelled from 5 minutes, a common prompt-cache lifetime.
